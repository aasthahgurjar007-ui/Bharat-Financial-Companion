01. Problem Understanding

The Personalization Void in Indian Banking

Indian banking is becoming increasingly digital, but personalization is often still product-first. Users receive generic offers even when their actual need is budgeting, saving, planning, understanding financial options, or managing financial stress.

PAIN POINTS What Users Experience Today
✕
Generic recommendations and repeated product offers: Constant personal loan and credit card pushes regardless of real user intent.
✕
Complex digital journeys and financial terminology: Intimidating jargon (APR, foreclosure charges, covenants) confuses first-time digital users.
✕
Recommendations based heavily on transactions: Algorithms observe transaction frequency while ignoring net disposable buffer and commitments.
✕
Financial stress becoming visible only after problems grow: Banks detect distress only upon bounced EMIs, triggering punitive recovery instead of early guidance.
OPPORTUNITY What Is Critically Missing
✓
A clear view of the customer's actual financial context: Accurate synthesis of true unencumbered surplus and recurring household dependencies.
✓
Simple, conversational and vernacular guidance: Natural conversations in native Hindi, Hinglish, and regional dialects that build user confidence.
✓
Understanding behaviour, goals, life stage and financial health: Aligning recommendations to life milestones rather than loan distribution targets.
✓
Early, non-judgmental signals and support: Proactive alerts that gently adjust budgets before mild strain turns into severe delinquency.
💡
Core Insight: Transaction Data Alone Is Not Enough.
Income, spending, savings, EMI/payment patterns, behaviour, goals and life-stage signals together create a much stronger picture of what a customer actually needs.
Target Users & Needs
Tier 2/3 and Rural Users
Intimidated by English-heavy portals; require vernacular voice/Hinglish coaching, visual aids, and micro-savings guidance.
Young Earners
First-job and gig earners earning ₹20k–₹50k with fluctuating expenses; vulnerable to impulse credit and unmanageable BNPL traps.
Goal-Oriented Customers
Individuals saving for life milestones (vehicle, emergency buffer, business inventory) requiring structured feasibility plans.

NLP & VOICE

✦ Voice and text copilot with culturally attuned analogies
Algorithmic Financial Health Formulation (0–100 Weighted Index)
• EMI Burden (25%): Ratio of total EMIs to net income (< 30% ideal)
• Savings Rate (20%): Consistent monthly surplus saved (> 15% ideal)
• Expense Ratio (20%): Essential vs discretionary spends (< 65% ideal)
• Emergency Cushion (15%): Liquid reserves covering 3–6 months spends
• Income Stability (10%): Regularity and source volatility index
• Credit Discipline (10%): Timely repayment record & zero defaults

05. Signature User Journey

Example: Meena – Young Working Customer

A step-by-step walkthrough demonstrating how Bharat Financial Companion converts an impulsive credit decision into an explainable, empowering life milestone.

Customer Persona: Meena (Age 24)

STEP 1
UNDERSTAND: The system builds Meena’s financial profile.
The system analyzes income regularity, essential living spends (₹23,000), recurring monthly savings (₹5,000), and an existing smartphone EMI of ₹4,000. It computes unencumbered monthly liquidity at ₹3,000.
STEP 2
ANALYSE: Goal feasibility is assessed using income, expenses, savings and existing EMI.
A ₹85,000 scooter with standard 2-year financing requires ₹4,100/mo EMI. Adding this to Meena's existing ₹4,000 EMI pushes total EMI to ₹8,100 (23.1% of income), exceeding her ₹3,000 net free buffer and jeopardizing emergency readiness.
STEP 3
RESPOND: AI identifies the goal feasibility as Moderate and explains the key financial constraint.
The engine marks goal feasibility as “MODERATE WITH CAUTION”. The AI transparently communicates: “Existing smartphone EMI of ₹4,000 restricts new monthly borrowing until month 4, when the phone loan matures.”
STEP 4
OFFER OPTIONS: The system provides multiple options:
Option 1: Save for 6 Months
Save ₹4,500/mo after phone EMI ends. A 35% down-payment reduces the loan to ₹50k, dropping EMI to ₹2,350/mo.
Option 2: Lower Loan Amount
Consider a certified electric/pre-owned scooter (₹55,000), bringing the EMI safely to ₹2,400/mo immediately.
Option 3: Compare Affordable EMIs
Compare 36-month tenure options at ₹2,750/mo EMI, keeping monthly outgo within Meena's ₹3,000 buffer.
STEP 5
EXPLAIN: Every recommendation is connected to Meena’s actual financial context.
Rather than a generic product push, AI Assistant explains: “Meena ji, abhi ₹4,100 ki nayi EMI lene se aapka monthly budget tight ho jayega kyunki phone ki EMI chal rahi hai. Agar aap 3 mahine ruk kar ₹15,000 down-payment save karein, toh scooter bhi aayega aur emergency savings bhi safe rahegi!”
AI CONVERSATIONAL INTERACTION (IN-APP HINGLISH VIEW)
Meena: “Mujhe agle mahine scooter lena hai ₹85,000 ka. Kya meri salary se loan manage ho jayega?”
Bharat Companion: “Meena ji, aapki monthly bachat ₹5,000 hai aur phone ki ₹4,000 EMI abhi 3 mahine bachi hai. Agar abhi ₹4,100 ki nayi EMI li, toh aapka kharcha budget se bahar ho jayega. Humne aapke liye 3 safe options banaye hain—Option 1 chunenge toh ₹12,000 interest bhi bachega!”
Why This Journey Matters
A single journey demonstrates: Personalization + Responsible Guidance + Explainability + Goal Planning.

06. AI / ML Approach

Pragmatic, Explainable & Scalable AI

The prototype will use a practical and explainable AI approach rather than attempting to train a complex foundation model from scratch. Deterministic financial scoring combines with machine learning segmentation and vernacular NLP.

INPUT SIGNALS
• Income: Regularity & volatility index
• Transactions: Frequency & velocity
• Spending Categories: Essential vs luxury
• Savings: Liquid balances & deposit cadence
• EMI/Payment Patterns: Debt service ratio
• Goals: Target amount & priority horizon
• Life-Stage Signals: Career stage & family needs
AI / LOGIC ENGINES
• Behavioural Segmentation: Persona clusters
• Recommendation Engine: Decision matrices
• Anomaly Detection: Isolation Forests
• NLP Assistant: Hindi/Hinglish intent parser
• Rule + Score Model: 0–100 health index
• Feasibility Solver: Constraint simulator
• Ethical Guardrails: Over-leverage filter
SYSTEM OUTPUTS
• Customer Profile: Holistic financial persona
• Financial Health Score: 0–100 gauge
• Goal Feasibility: High / Moderate / Tight
• Explainable Recommendations: Why & Why Now
• Stress Alerts: Non-judgmental early warnings
• Conversational Guidance: Vernacular advice
• Actionable Plans: Safe milestone roadmaps
BEHAVIOURAL SEGMENTATION & ANOMALY
Clusters users into spending personas using RFM and Isolation Forests. Flags sudden drops in balance or spikes in high-interest debt without human bias.
EXPLAINABLE RECOMMENDATION ENGINE
Rule + scoring model determines next best actions. Every suggestion links back to mathematical cash buffers, eliminating opaque algorithmic black boxes.
